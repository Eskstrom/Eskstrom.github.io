One journey across two devices

Seven mobile frames separate finding a court, choosing a time, reviewing the booking, confirmation, management, and cancellation. A smartwatch companion explores the same core task in a smaller interaction space.

A deliberate cancellation

The documented smartwatch check follows booking through management, deliberate cancellation, and a cleared booking count. On mobile, the cancellation-result frame still needs its remaining confirmation copy and actions corrected.

What comes next

Finish mobile frame review and prototype connections; verify duplicate handling, recovery, sharing, and calendar export on the watch. The fixed sample date and ambiguous court label also need correction before a final presentation.
