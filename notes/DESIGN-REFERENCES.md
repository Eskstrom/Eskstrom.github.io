# Design direction

Reviewed 21 September 2026.

- [Srikrishnan Srinivasa](https://srikrishnansrinivasa.github.io/): the user-provided reference for clear positioning, dark surfaces, deliberate typography, section hierarchy, and direct contact. No personal claims, wording, or assets are copied.
- [Brittany Chiang](https://brittanychiang.com/): readable dark surfaces, restrained navigation, and a focus on project evidence.
- [Bruno Simon](https://bruno-simon.com/): gaming personality and exploration. Here the game-library approach keeps projects reachable through ordinary navigation.
- [Astro Bento Portfolio](https://github.com/Ladvace/astro-bento-portfolio): considered for compact project cards. No template code copied.

Direction: graphite surfaces, electric lime actions, large headings, project previews, a browsable library, and readable case-study pages. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Journey diagrams summarize documented flows and are explicitly distinct from original design screenshots.
